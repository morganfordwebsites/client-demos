FAQ
What ID do I bring?

A current, government-issued photo ID — driver's license, state ID, passport, or military ID — for every signer.

Can a notary give legal advice?

No. Notaries verify identity and witness signatures. For legal guidance, consult a licensed attorney.

How does remote notarization work?

You receive a secure link for a video session, verify your identity with photo ID, sign electronically, and we apply a digital seal. Ten to fifteen minutes, typically.

Must I sign in front of the notary?

Yes, for most documents. Bring them unsigned; we will indicate exactly when to sign.

How long does an appointment take?

Fifteen to twenty minutes for most documents; loan packages typically run sixty to ninety minutes.

How do I pay, and what is the cancellation policy?
