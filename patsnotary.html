About Patricia

A teacher's precision, a veteran's word.

“I have very high standards, and I’m detail-oriented.”

Patricia Thurman was born in Greensboro and knows the surrounding areas of Guilford County like few others. An Army veteran and retired high school business teacher, she was commissioned as a North Carolina Notary Public in March 2025 and certified by the National Notary Association as a signing agent.

Services

Notary work that travels to you.

From a single signature to a full loan package, Patricia brings the seal, the ledger and the laser printer — so the signing happens on your schedule, not an office's.

Loan Document Signings

As a Certified Signing Agent, Patricia handles full real-estate and refinance packages — printed, organized, and returned correctly the first time. Title companies, signing services and lenders can rely on a detail-oriented closer.
