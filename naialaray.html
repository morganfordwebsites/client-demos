03 · About Nai'ala

A notary with a real estate eye.

I'm a commissioned Indiana Notary Public working out of Indianapolis. Before and alongside notary work, I've spent time in real estate, which means I know what's actually at stake in a loan package, and why a missed initial or an undated certificate can stall a closing.

I'm organized, punctual, and careful with what you hand me. Documents stay confidential, appointments start on time, and every signing is checked for signatures, dates, and initials before I leave the room.

"Every signing completed correctly the first time."

Whether you're an individual who needs one document notarized or a title company sending a full purchase package, the standard is the same: accurate, professional, and stress-free.
