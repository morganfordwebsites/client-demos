The person
at the table.

Ten years of closings taught me that a signing is rarely about the paperwork. It is a refinance a couple has been chasing for months, a reverse mortgage that finally lets a parent stay in her home, or a power of attorney signed at a hospital bed.

I am highly organized and reliable. I print my own packages and I arrive early. I work in English, Russian and Ukrainian, which matters in a county where a signer understands the note far better in a first language. I have executed a large number of remote closings, and I stay calm when a package reaches the table with pages missing.

In business on my own since June 2019, covering Miami-Dade from a mobile office: printer, scanner and hotspot in the car.
