How I Work

No sleaze. No runaround. Just cars.

Adam runs the lot himself, and he'd rather you leave happy than leave pressured.

01

Transparent & open

Buying a used car can be imposing. How do you know you're getting a good deal, and how can you be sure the car you buy is safe and reliable? At Motivated Motors the process is transparent and open: every customer leaves satisfied with paying a fair price for a quality, reliable vehicle. "It's that simple."

02

Take your time

If you're looking for a sleazy sales associate or a hard sell, look elsewhere. That means no pushing for a sale. Take a test drive, ask questions, and have some time to think. Whether it's a sports car or a daily driver, you'll get straight answers.

03

A bit car-mad

It wouldn't be unfair to say Adam's a bit car-mad. That passion means he can guide you through the buying process, whether it's your first car or your 40th, and help you find your perfect wheels at a price that's unbeatable. Talk your requirements through and book a test drive.
