New service · Field inspections

Eyes on the property, report filed the same day.

A signing happens at a table. A field inspection happens at the curb, the front door, and the meter on the side of the house. Lenders, servicers, and asset managers order them to confirm a property is still what the file says it is, and somebody has to go look.

That is now part of the work here. Same thirty-mile radius, same car, same habit of photographing things properly the first time so nobody has to send the order back.

  • Occupancy checksWhether the property is occupied, vacant, or abandoned, and what on the exterior suggests which.
  • Property conditionCondition reports covering damage, deferred maintenance, and anything that has changed since the last visit.
  • Loss & damageHail, wind, fire, and water damage documented with dated photographs and measurements where the order asks for them.
  • Photo documentationWide shots and close shots, GPS-stamped and named the way the servicer's order expects to receive them.

How an order runs

Sent by email or through the servicer's portal.

  1. Address, order number, and the scope of the inspection confirmed in writing.
  2. Visit scheduled to the order's due date, with a call ahead where the property is occupied.
  3. Photographs and the completed report returned the same day.
  4. Any condition that needs a phone call gets one before the report closes.
