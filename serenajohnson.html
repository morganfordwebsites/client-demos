How it works

How a signing bends.

Three steps. No forms to chase, no folder to drive across town.

01

Tell me the document and the address

Text a photo if you have one. That's enough for me to say whether it needs a notary, a witness, or something else entirely.

02

Get one flat quote before the appointment

Ask for the number up front, so nothing moves when I arrive. No meter running, no surprise line items at the door.

03

I show up with the printer and the stamp

We sign, I check every notarial block, and you keep a clean copy. If a page is missing, it gets printed on the spot.

The paper trail

The stamp has a record behind it.

South Carolina notary commissions come through the Secretary of State's office downtown, and that's the standard every signature here is held to: confirm who the signer is, watch the signature go down, log the entry. No shortcuts, and never a notarization for somebody who isn't in the room.
