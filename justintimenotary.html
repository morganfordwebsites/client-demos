Services

What gets signed and stamped

Bring the document, a valid government photo ID, and everyone who needs to sign. Leave the signature line blank until we meet.

  • 01

    Mobile notary & document signing

    Drive up to Atalon Dr in Moore, or have the notary come to your home, office, or wherever the signer is. Scheduled, walk-in, and late-evening visits are all on the menu.

    Affidavits · Acknowledgments · Sworn statements

  • 02

    Loan & real estate closings

    NNA certified signing agent with a dual-tray laser printer and a mobile scanner, so letter and legal pages print in order and scan-backs are handled on site.

    Purchases · Refinances · Seller packages

  • 03

    Estate planning & powers of attorney

    Notarization for the documents your attorney or family has prepared. A notary can't draft them or give legal advice, but can make sure they are signed and sealed correctly.

    Powers of attorney · Trust documents · Healthcare directives

  • 04

    Remote / virtual notarization options

    Can't meet in person? Call or text to ask whether your document qualifies for a remote signing and how it would work.

    Ask when you call
