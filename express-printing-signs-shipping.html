01 · The shop

A mother, a daughter, eighteen years of ink.

One storefront on East Baltimore Street has produced the yard signs, the church banners, the team shirts and the business cards behind a good deal of downtown Jackson. Two people started it, and they still run it.

Express Printing, Signs and Shipping opened on August 8, 2008, in downtown Jackson, started by Susan Stewart and her daughter Brandy. The two had worked together before they owned anything together, and they have been at it side by side ever since.

A business loan through the Southwest Tennessee Development District paid for equipment that let the shop stop outsourcing work and start finishing it in house. That is the reason a set of business forms, a run of aluminum signs and a stack of shirts can all leave from the same counter, usually without a middleman in between.

In 2018 the Southwest Tennessee Development District named them Small Business of the Year. In 2019, Jackson's mayor handed them the trophy for the best decorated business downtown, which is a fair description of a shop that spends its days making other storefronts look good.

Most people can't work with their parents, but we understand each other. Susan Stewart, to Downtown Jackson TN

Questions the counter gets

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 5:30. For a large sign job or a shirt order, a phone call ahead is a good idea so the machine time can be set aside.

Can you notarize a document?

Yes, there is a notary at the shop, and it is one of the things people come in for most. One reviewer needed a document notarized in a hurry and was taken care of right away. Bring the document and your photo ID, as with any notary.

Do you design it, or do I bring artwork?

Either. Graphic design for marketing materials is one of the shop's services, so a flyer, a card or a banner can be laid out here, or built from the file you bring.

How long does an order take?

It depends on the job, the stock and the queue. Call (731) 421-8966 with your date and you will get a straight answer before you commit.

Do you ship packages as well as print?

Yes. Packing and shipping have been part of the business from the start, alongside mailing and package services at the same counter.
