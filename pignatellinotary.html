About

Built on service discipline.

Pignatelli CT Mobile Notary is a one-man operation in Colchester, built on the habits that come out of a military background: be early, be exact, sign nothing you haven't read.

Spenser Pignatelli spent his service years in an environment where a missed line on a form had consequences, and that is roughly the whole story of how he notarizes today. As a commissioned Connecticut Notary Public and a Notary Public Association Certified Notary Signing Agent, he works a schedule that looks less like a storefront and more like a route: closings in the morning, hospitals and care facilities in the afternoon, and whatever needs a notary after that.

The work splits cleanly. Loan signings and real estate closings are the primary practice — loan packages and attorney closings where the file has to come back complete, legible and correctly dated. General notarization covers the rest: acknowledgments, jurats, oaths, powers of attorney, affidavits and certified copies, usually in small batches at a kitchen table.

He arrives equipped rather than improvising. A laser printer and scanner travel with him, so a missing page gets printed and a completed package gets scanned before he leaves the room. A mobile hotspot covers e-signing and EscrowTab IPEN work when there's no usable Wi-Fi. Fingerprinting, weddings, hospital signings and jail signings are all regular parts of the service list, and he is RON capable for remote signers who can't be present in person.

Coverage runs from the five core towns around Colchester out across Connecticut by appointment, including access to military installations — a detail that matters to the veteran and military families he already serves, and one that very few mobile notaries can offer at all. He is NNA certified, background screened and carries a $100,000 errors and omissions policy, so the agency, title company or attorney sending the package has something real to point at.
