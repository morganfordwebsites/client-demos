01 · What we clean

Six surfaces. Two methods. Usually one visit.

Concrete gets pressure. Anything that can crack, lift or soak through gets soft washing: cleaning agents and low pressure. We look at the surface before we pick a nozzle.

  • 01

    Driveways & concrete

    The grey film, oil spots, rust and tire marks that Maryland winters leave behind, gone down to clean aggregate.
  • 02

    Patios & pavers

    Deep clean, then re-sand the joints and seal, so weeds stop finding a home and the colour holds another season.
  • 03

    Walkways, steps & brick

    Front-entry concrete, bluestone and brick brought back one pass at a time. Consistent pressure, no etched lines.
  • 04

    Siding, vinyl to cedar

    Soft washing for vinyl siding, aluminum siding and cedar. Cleaning agents do the work, so nothing gets forced behind a panel.
  • 05

    Roofs, soft washed

    The dark streaking on a north-facing roof is algae, not dirt. Low pressure and a cleaning solution, never a jet on shingles.
  • 06

    Decks & fences

    Clean and brighten cedar or pressure-treated wood, then seal it. Loose boards and rail work are on the same truck.

06 · Questions

The five we get asked most.

Anything else, the phone is faster. It is answered around the clock.

Call (301) 674-8472
Will a pressure washer hurt my siding?+
Not the way we work. Siding, cedar and roofs get soft washing: cleaning agents and low pressure doing the work. High pressure stays on concrete, brick, pavers and stone, where it belongs.
How do you handle the roof streaks?+
Soft roof washing. The dark streaking is algae growing on the shingle, and a cleaning solution kills it and lets the rain rinse it away. No surface pressure, so no lifted or bruised shingles.
Do you seal pavers and decks?+
Yes. Paver cleaning with re-sanding and sealing, and deck cleaning with sealing, are both standard jobs for us. Sealing needs a dry surface, so it is usually booked a day or two after the wash.
How soon can you come out?+
It depends on the week, so call and we will tell you the next open slot honestly instead of promising a day we cannot hold. The line is open 24/7.
How do I pay?+
Credit or debit card, and the other usual ways too. Ask on the call and we will set it up before we start, so nothing slows down at the end.
