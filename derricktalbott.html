07

Straight answers

The questions that come up on the phone, answered before you have to ask.

What do I need to have ready?
A valid, unexpired government photo ID for every signer. Do not sign anything in advance — the whole point is that he watches the pen move. Have the full document in hand, including any riders or exhibits.
How does the travel fee work?
Flat rates: $75 within 2 miles, $100 up to 10 miles. Past 10 miles it's $100 plus $2.50 for each additional mile. Stamping at his North Baldwin office is $2 per document. The number is quoted when you book, not sprung on you at the door.
Can he work from e-docs at the table?
Yes. He takes e-docs, fax docs, digital fax and overnight packages, and prints on site with a laser printer if a page needs to be redone. He also carries a mobile hotspot for buildings without signal.
What if the signer can't travel?
That's most of the work. Hospital rooms, nursing homes, assisted living and correctional facilities are all routine. If the signer is out of state entirely, remote online notarization (RON) through EscrowTab or an IPEN platform covers it.
How late can he come out?
Appointments run Monday through Saturday, 9am to 10pm. The cell line is answered around the clock for after-hours emergencies: (516) 659-1392.
Something changed — how do I move the appointment?
Call or text the cell at (516) 659-1392. Moving a slot is easy when it's not the same hour as the signing.
Still not sure
Tell him what the document is. He'll tell you what it needs.

Fewer than half the signings start from a booked slot. People call, describe the paperwork, and get a time in the same minute.
