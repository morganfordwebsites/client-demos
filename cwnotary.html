III — About Carla

Thirty-eight years of handling documents that matter.

"My experience as a notary fits well with my customer service experience. I am known for my attention to detail and professionalism."

Carla Williams

Carla Williams retired from the federal government after more than 38 years of service, including over 20 years in property management, work where a single missed signature can delay a closing, a lease, or a claim.

She also spent five-plus years in a car dealership's finance department, preparing auto loans and the legal and financial documents that ride along with them. She knows what lenders and title companies look for in a completed package, because she spent a career on the other side of the table.

Today she brings that same care to south Alabama as an NNA-certified Notary Signing Agent, with an active background check through Accurate Background and an Alabama commission valid through November 2029.
