The work behind the name.

In today's business climate, administrative and human resource functions have become increasingly difficult to navigate, especially for ministries, non-profits and small businesses. Some have had to close their doors, or shift focus away from their mission, because of the time and resources it takes to wade through the complexity of these functions.

Functions that should follow, rather than lead.

That is the problem HR Made Simpler was built around. Jordana's passion and expertise is in helping organisations align day to day tasks with their mission statement and goals: simplifying processes, policies and procedures, and hiring the right people to carry them.

In addition to education and certifications, she brings more than 28 years of HR, operations and administrative experience. Over that time she has provided consultation for many ministries, businesses and individuals, making positive changes that simplified these functions and gave the people inside them room to do their actual job.

With determination and a service-minded spirit, she welcomes the opportunity to learn the heart you have for your business, and to partner with you to make the complex simpler again.
