02 / The premise

A poor culture or a compliance gap can threaten the livelihood of a mission.

So the framework has to be right: hiring the right people, and effective systems, policies and procedures underneath them. That is what makes employee satisfaction, positive culture, retention and continuity possible.

Administrative and HR functions have become harder to navigate than the work they are meant to support. Too often they end up leading the organisation instead of following it. The job here is to put them back in their place.
