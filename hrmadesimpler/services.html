01 / Recruitment

Taking the legwork out of hiring

From the decision to hire through to a compliant applicant file, the whole sequence is run for you. Your team meets shortlisted candidates, not a stack of resumes.

Start with a consultation
  • Assessment of the position needed
  • Job description creation
  • Ad creation and posting
  • Candidate screening
  • Phone interviews
  • Interview scheduling with your team
  • Background checks
  • Compliant applicant record keeping

Background checks and applicant records are handled to the standard an auditor would expect, which matters most in organizations that have never had an HR department.

03 / Employee handbook

Policy that reflects the mission

A complete audit and creation, or a rewrite of what already exists, for compliance, policy and mission fit.

  • Audit of the current handbook
  • Compliance review and corrections
  • New handbook written from scratch
  • Rewrite of existing policy language
  • Alignment with mission and culture
  • Policies your board can defend

Most handbooks fail in one of two ways: they copy a template from another industry, or they were written once and never revisited. Both are fixable.
