The general inspection

Eight systems.
One written record.

A standard inspection walks the whole house in a fixed order, so nothing gets skipped and nothing gets guessed at. Each finding below goes into the report with a photo and a note on why it matters.

01

Roof & attic

Shingle condition, flashing, valleys, decking, attic ventilation and insulation depth — the places a leak starts long before it shows up on a ceiling.

02

Structure & foundation

Slab and footings, framing, piers, crawlspace, and the grading and drainage that decide whether water stays outside the house.

03

Electrical

Service drop and meter, panel and breakers, wiring methods, grounding, and GFCI/AFCI protection where the code expects it.

04

Plumbing

Supply lines, drains and vents, water heater and its relief valve, fixtures, and the shutoffs you'll want to know about on move-in day.

05

Heating & cooling

Furnace or air handler, condenser, ductwork, flue, condensate handling, and the age and service history of the equipment.

06

Exterior

Siding, trim, windows and doors, decks and railings, driveways, walkways, and the drainage that ties them together.

07

Interior

Walls, ceilings, floors, stairs and handrails, interior doors, and any evidence of past moisture or movement.

08

Site & grounds

Grading against the foundation, downspout discharge, retaining walls, fences, and outbuildings on the property.

Beyond the general report

Thirteen specialty
inspections.

Some problems don't show up in a visual walkthrough. G&S Enterprises offers the add-on services that test for them directly, so a question about radon or a septic field gets answered before closing rather than after.

Radon testing

Test

Short-term measurement of radon gas, the leading cause of lung cancer among non-smokers and invisible without a test.

Mold inspection

Test

Moisture mapping and sampling to separate a surface stain from a growth problem worth pricing into the deal.

Septic system

Test

Tank, baffles and drain field reviewed on rural properties outside city sewer.

Sewer line scope

Camera

Camera run through the lateral to find root intrusion, bellies, offsets and cracked pipe underground.

Well inspection

Test

Pressure, equipment and water quality for homes on a private well.

Thermal imaging

Infrared

Infrared camera work that reads temperature differences: moisture intrusion, missing insulation, overheating components.

Chimney inspection

Visual

Firebox, damper, flue and cap, with creosote and clearance concerns called out.

Pools & spas

Visual

Shell, pumps, filters, heaters, bonding and the safety features around the water.

Lead testing

Test

Paint and surface testing for older housing stock, where lead risk is highest.

Meth testing

Thermal imaging

The wall tells you
what the eye can't.

An infrared camera doesn't see through drywall — it reads surface temperature. A wet patch, a missing batt of insulation or a hot breaker reads several degrees off from everything around it, and that difference shows up as a shape on screen.

01Moisture intrusion behind ceilings, around windows and at roof penetrations
02Gaps in insulation and the air leaks that drive a winter power bill
03Overheating breakers, connections and equipment before they fail
