About

A Milwaukee notary who comes to you.

Since 2016, Chambers Notary & Process Service has handled the documents Milwaukee families, attorneys and businesses can't afford to get wrong — from wills and powers of attorney to loan signings, evictions and court filings.

Owner Monique Chambers is a certified mobile notary and loan signing agent who brings the office to your home, workplace, care facility or job site — with same-day and after-hours appointments whenever the schedule allows.

Need papers served? Her process serving team covers all of Wisconsin, with standard, rush and same-day options.
