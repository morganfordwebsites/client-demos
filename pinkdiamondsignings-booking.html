Good to know

Questions, answered.

What ID do I bring?+
A current, government-issued photo ID — driver's license, state ID, passport, or military ID — for every signer.
Can a notary give legal advice?+
No. Notaries verify identity and witness signatures. For legal guidance, consult a licensed attorney.
How does remote notarization work?+
You receive a secure video link, verify identity with photo ID, sign electronically, and we apply a digital seal. Typically ten to fifteen minutes.
Must I sign in front of the notary?+
Yes, for most documents. Bring them unsigned — we'll indicate exactly when to sign.
How long does an appointment take?+
Fifteen to twenty minutes for most documents; loan packages typically sixty to ninety.
How do I pay, and what's the cancellation policy?+
Cash, Zelle, Venmo, or card at the time of service. Cancel or reschedule at least 4 hours ahead at no charge; later cancellations may incur a $15 trip fee.
