Questions Alena answers weekly.

What do I need to bring?

A current, unexpired government photo ID (driver’s license, state ID, passport, or military ID). Bring the complete document, unsigned, plus any instructions your lender, attorney, or agency sent you. Everyone who needs to sign must be present with their own ID.

Can you come to my house, a hospital, or a care facility?

Yes, that is most of the work. Alena travels to homes, ranches, hospitals, care facilities, and title offices throughout the Arkansas Valley. Hospital and bedside signings are routine; mention it when you call so she can plan around visiting hours.

How late can I book?

Alena offers 24-hour and after-hours service, including weekends and holidays. Evening and same-day call-outs carry the $30 rush fee. If it is after 9pm, send a text and she will confirm first thing in the morning.

How far will you travel?

Cotopaxi, Cañon City, Westcliffe, and the surrounding routes are standard. Beyond that, travel is $50 for the first 30 miles and $20 for each additional 20, plus mileage from $0.50 to $1.00 per mile, always quoted before you book.

Do you handle loan signings for title companies?

Yes. Alena is a certified signing agent, Fidelity approved, and works purchases, refinances, HELOCs, reverse mortgages, and seller packages. She prints, presents, and returns the package with a scan-back the same day.

Is remote online notarization available?

Alena is RON capable and set up for e-signing, which works well for signers who are out of state or unable to meet in person. Ask about it when you call.

¿Se habla español?

Sí. Alena atiende a sus clientes en inglés y en español, y explica cada página antes de firmar.
