About Sand County Environmental

Founded in Rosholt, Wisconsin in 1996. Four offices, more than 120 active projects, and staff who have been doing this work for decades.

Sand County Environmental, Inc. was established in 1996 as a start-up company. It grew from a small regional firm into a practice running more than 120 active projects across the country, with primary offices in Rosholt and Rhinelander, Wisconsin, and staff in Plymouth and Johnson Creek.

The team is made up of professionals in geology, engineering, soil science, hydrogeology and biology. Most staff have more than twenty years of professional consulting experience, and many have been with the firm for a decade or more.

The company traded for years as Sand Creek Consultants, Inc. and now operates as Sand County Environmental, Inc. Work is most often conducted for owners of landfills, lenders, retail and wholesale agricultural chemical storage facilities, local and state governments, and other property owners where soil, groundwater or stormwater management is a concern.

We are known among clients and peers for solving complex geologic and regulatory problems, for phytotechnology, and for making technical material comprehensible to the people who have to sign off on it.
