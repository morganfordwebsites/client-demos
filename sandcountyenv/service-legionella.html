Legionella Testing

Potable water testing for Legionella bacteria, lead, copper and total coliform, including E. coli.

Under the federal requirement for all GSA-leased facilities, Sand County Environmental can test drinking water systems for Legionella bacteria, lead, copper and total coliform bacteria, including E. coli.

We sample using approved techniques and state-approved laboratories, and we help you prepare the facility for sampling, report the methods and results properly, and understand what the findings mean.

What we do

  • Legionella bacteria testing
  • Lead and copper testing
  • Total coliform and E. coli
  • Facility preparation guidance
  • Approved sampling methods
  • Results reporting and interpretation
