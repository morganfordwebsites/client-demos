Eight practices, one case file.

Most clients arrive with one problem and leave with a file that touches several of these. What follows is what the work actually involves, not a list of slogans.

01

Phytoremediation & phytotechnology

Plant-based systems that use hybrid poplar and willow to treat leachate, groundwater, stormwater, wastewater effluent and impacted soil. We carry a project from site characterisation through feasibility testing, design, permitting and installation, then operate and monitor the stand for as long as the file stays open. We also provide consulting and installation support to other engineering firms where phytotechnology is the required sub-discipline.

Applied at landfills, dry cleaners, refineries, ag-chem terminals and Superfund sites in 20+ states

  • In-situ and ex-situ systems
  • Feasibility and bench-scale testing
  • Clone selection and planting design
  • Permitting and regulatory approval
  • Irrigation, operations and monitoring
  • Alternative and hybrid poplar landfill covers
02

Solid waste & landfill services

Monitoring and remediation for closed and active landfills: groundwater, surface water and landfill gas, leachate management, gas migration control and alternative covers. Work is performed to WDNR rules, often on monitoring programmes that have run for decades.

Applied at a 40-acre closed town landfill complex; a paper company landfill in North Central Wisconsin

  • Groundwater, surface water and gas monitoring
  • Leachate collection and on-site treatment
  • Soil vapour extraction and passive venting
  • Hybrid poplar and alternative covers
  • Closure reporting and case closure
  • Regional integrated solid waste planning
03

Agricultural-chemical facilities

More than 265 ag-chem handling facilities have come through this practice, from liquid fertiliser terminals on the Mississippi to small agronomy co-ops. We know how these sites are built, how they fail, and what the regulator will accept as a remedy.

Applied at a 150,000-pound subsurface nitrogen release; a banned dinoseb stockpile at a northern Wisconsin airport

  • Containment structure investigation
  • Nitrogen and pesticide soil remediation
  • Groundwater investigation and response
  • ACCP case management and closure
  • Facility design and stormwater management
  • Spill response support
04

Environmental site assessment

ASTM Phase I and Phase II Environmental Site Assessments and due diligence for property transfers, plus soil, groundwater and vapour investigation, remedial action reporting, post-remediation monitoring and regulatory closure. Reports are written to be read by a lender, a buyer and a regulator without a translator.

Applied at a 50-year record review for a river terminal in Minnesota; brownfield, foundry and drycleaner closures

  • ASTM Phase I ESA
  • Phase II investigation work plans
  • Soil, groundwater and vapour sampling
  • Remedial action reporting
  • Post-remediation monitoring and closure
  • Due diligence for lenders and buyers
05

Stormwater

Evaluation, design, permitting and construction oversight for industrial, commercial and land-disturbing sites. WinSLAMM and HydroCAD modelling, pond permitting, NOI, erosion control and stormwater management plans. Where the site allows it, we design and build the treatment system too.

Applied at a DATCP-overseen willow basin system; a lined detention pond cutting total suspended solids by 44%

  • Stormwater modelling and design
  • Pond and outfall permitting
  • NOI and WPDES compliance
  • Erosion control and management plans
  • Construction oversight
  • Plant-based treatment basins
06

Wastewater & engineered wetlands

Alternative treatment systems and constructed wetlands that treat leachate, wastewater and stormwater through plants, soil and microbes, as a sole remedy or as one component of a multi-remedy strategy.

Applied at tertiary wastewater treatment; stream protection under the Clean Water Act

  • Engineered treatment wetlands
  • Tertiary wastewater treatment
  • Leachate pre-treatment
  • Pollutant attenuation design
  • Planting and establishment
  • Long-term performance monitoring
07

Wetlands, PFAS & compliance

Wetland delineation and restoration, shoreland permitting, PFAS leachate pre-treatment design, Legionella, asbestos inspections, hazardous materials and RCRA compliance, SPCC plans and stack test data entry. Staff hold PG, PE, PSS, CHMM, TSSA and Certified Asbestos Inspector credentials.

Applied at wetland delineation on a 1.8-acre construction site; PFAS removal from landfill leachate and well water

  • Wetland delineation and permitting
  • Wetland restoration
  • Shoreland permitting
  • PFAS leachate and well-water treatment
  • Asbestos inspections and Hazmat & RCRA compliance
  • SPCC plans and stack test data entry (ERT)
08

Litigation support & expert testimony

Historical operations reconstruction, groundwater record synthesis, expert reports and testimony. We turn decades of disorganised paper into one coherent, digitally managed case file that counsel can actually work from.

Applied at a paper mill groundwater and landfill case: 1,000+ pre-trial hours across 85 years of records

  • Historical operations reconstruction
  • Groundwater record synthesis
  • Expert reports
  • Deposition and trial testimony
  • Regulatory negotiation support
  • Peer review of opposing technical work
