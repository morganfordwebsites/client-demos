How it works

From your old system to a booked Tuesday.

Most operators are running on paper, a spreadsheet and a memory. Switching takes one to two weeks, and we do the part everyone dreads: the data.

Start a switch

01 / Onboarding

Three steps, and none of them are yours to build.

Included in the price. No setup fee, no contract.

01

Export and clean

If your data lives in another system, we help export it. Then we clean it: duplicate properties, missing tank sizes, old balances, bad phone numbers. You review the result before it goes live.

Step one
02

Live training

One to four sessions with the office and the drivers. We configure your services, service cycles, invoice layout and reminders together, using your real jobs, not a demo file.

Step two
03

Launch

You start booking in Tank Track. The old system stays readable while the team gets comfortable. When you are ready, we help you turn it off.

Step three

02 / What moves over

The history you already earned.

If it is in your current system, ask us to bring it. Most of it can come across.

  • Customers and contactsNames, phones, emails, billing
  • Properties and sitesAddresses, access notes, gate codes
  • Tanks and equipmentSize, material, riser, last service
  • Service historyDates, gallons, work performed
  • Open balancesInvoices and credits
  • Service cyclesWho is due, and when

We do not charge for the import, the cleanup or the training sessions.

Follow-ups

04 / Questions

The things operators ask first.

Still stuck on something? Call (833) 826-5875 and a person will answer.

Do we have to enter every customer ourselves?
No. We export and clean your existing data and import it for you. You review it before anything goes live.
How long does setup take?
Onboarding runs through one to four live sessions. How long it takes depends on how clean your current data is, not on how many trucks you run.
Do drivers need special equipment?
Work orders, customer details and signatures live on the phone or tablet, so drivers are not carrying paper into the yard.
Do you charge per user?
No. Pricing is per truck. Add the whole office, every driver and every seasonal hire at no extra cost.
Is there a free trial?
Tank Track does not offer a free trial. We do a live walkthrough with your own jobs instead, so you can judge it on your routes rather than a demo file.
What happens if we want to leave?
There is no contract. Cancel and we will export your data for you. We would rather earn the renewal than trap you in one.
