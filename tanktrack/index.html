02 / Who it is for

Built for three trades that do not forgive a missed stop.

01

Septic

Pump outs and repairs

Tank size, last pump date, riser location and the note about the cracked lid all come up before the truck leaves the yard.

  • Service intervals by tank and household
  • Riser, lid and drain field notes
  • County-ready pumping records
02

Grease trap

Interceptors and manifests

Keep every interceptor on its required cycle, log the gallons pulled and hand the inspector a clean manifest without rebuilding it in a spreadsheet.

  • Trap size and pumping frequency
  • FOG disposal and hauling records
  • Multi-unit and chain properties
03

Portable restrooms

Drops, service and pickups

Units, sites and event windows in one place, so the same truck is not sent twice and the pickup does not wait on a phone call.

  • Unit counts by site and event
  • Delivery and pickup windows
  • Service tickets and damage notes
