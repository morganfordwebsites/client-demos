Features

Everything in one app.

Tank Track brings fuel management, content, subscriptions, payments, analytics and support together. Here is what each part does.

Six features Start with user management
01 / User management

Full control over every account.

Manage user accounts, profiles and access from one place. Edit details, disable an account or reset a password without leaving the app.

Administrators see the whole picture. Users see what they need. Access follows the role you give, not a separate spreadsheet kept somewhere else.

  • 1Roles and accessDecide who can view, edit and manage.
  • 2Profile editsKeep names, contact details and vehicles current.
  • 3Password resetsResolve access problems without a support ticket.

02 / Content management

Answers, where people look for them.

Manage and publish FAQs, tutorials and support articles inside the app. Keep the help center current so users can solve the small things without waiting for a reply.

When a process changes, update the article once. Everyone sees the same answer the next time they open it.

  • 1FAQsShort answers to the questions that come up most.
  • 2TutorialsWalk through fuel entries, trips, reports and payments.
  • 3Support articlesKeep policies and account guidance in one place.

03 / Subscription management

Know where every plan stands.

Keep track of subscriptions, monitor payment statuses and handle subscription questions with the full history in front of you.

Renewal dates, plan changes and cancellations are visible in the app, so a question about a charge does not need a search through email.

  • 1Plan statusActive, paused or cancelled, in one view.
  • 2RenewalsSee the next charge before it happens.
  • 3ChangesUpgrade, downgrade or cancel from the account.

04 / Payment management

Every payment, in one history.

Monitor payment transactions, access payment history and resolve payment queries efficiently. Card details stay with the payment provider; Tank Track keeps the record.

If a receipt is missing or a charge looks wrong, the answer is usually one screen away.

  • 1Transaction historyFuel, subscriptions and renewals in one list.
  • 2ReceiptsOpen the record for any payment.
  • 3StatusPaid, pending or refunded, clearly marked.

05 / Analytics and reports

Numbers that explain your month.

Fuel entries, trip logs, mileage and efficiency become reports you can read at a glance. See what you spent, how far you went and where the money went.

Reports are estimates based on the data you and your devices provide. They are there to make the month clearer, not to replace your own records.

  • 1Fuel spendMonth by month, with the average price you paid.
  • 2Mileage and efficiencyDistance, mpg and cost per mile.
  • 3Monthly reportsA clean summary you can read in a minute.

06 / User support

Help that stays in the app.

Support tickets and in-app messages sit beside your account, so a question does not get lost in an inbox. Our team is here every step of the way.

Start with the help center. If the answer is not there, open a ticket and keep the conversation with the account it belongs to.

  • 1Support ticketsOpen, track and resolve in one thread.
  • 2In-app messagesAsk without leaving the screen you are on.
  • 3Help centerAnswers for the questions that come up most.
