Pricing questions

Asked and answered.

Need a number for a specific fleet? Call and we will work it out on the phone.

What counts as a truck?
A truck is a service vehicle running routes in Tank Track. The first one is $180 a month and each additional truck is $150 a month.
How does annual billing work?
Annual billing takes 10% off the monthly rate and is paid once a year. There is still no contract, and the plan is still priced per truck.
Is there a setup or onboarding fee?
No. Data export, cleanup, import and the live training sessions are included.
Do you charge extra for users or storage?
No. Users, tanks and storage are unlimited on every plan.
Do you offer a free trial?
No. Instead we do a live walkthrough with your own routes and data, so you can see how it behaves on your business before you commit.
What if we add or drop a truck?
Pricing follows the trucks you are running. Tell us when the fleet changes and the plan is adjusted from there. No long term commitment either way.
