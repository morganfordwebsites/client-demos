What’s actually happening

Every couple has a loop.

Here’s what yours probably looks like. It usually starts smaller than anyone expects. A dish. A tone. A text left on read. By the end you’re not fighting about that anymore, you’re fighting about how you fight.

  1. 01

    Something tiny

    A dish in the sink. A sigh. A “nothing.” Nobody thinks this is the fight yet.

  2. 02

    The armor goes on

    One of you gets sharp. The other goes flat. Both are trying to stay safe.

  3. 03

    You’re off the topic

    The dish is gone. Now it’s about who always does this, and who never listens.

  4. 04

    The silence

    Nothing resolved. Someone sleeps on the couch. The thing goes underground.

  5. 05

    The patch

    You make up. It feels better. It is not fixed. It waits, and Tuesday comes again.

You’re not bad at love. You’re running a pattern nobody taught you to see. My whole job is helping you find yours, name it, and interrupt it.

About Ed

I’m a tour guide, not a guru.

Hi, I’m Ed Riddick. I’m a counselor in private practice, and my expertise is communication, conflict resolution and anger management. I’ve been coaching couples for over 30 years.

I hold a Master’s in Counseling from Trinity International University and a Master’s in Dispute Resolution from Marquette University. My focus is communication and conflict resolution for couples. I’m a neutral third party, and both women and men tend to be comfortable with what I share. I’m as at ease in an auto repair shop or on a job site as I am in my office.

It wasn’t long into my practice before I realized I also needed specific training in anger management. (Who would have thought anger was part of relational conflict.) I’m a Certified Anger Management Specialist–II, and I help clients learn to manage theirs.

I delight in helping couples break free from the death spiral of serial arguing. As the cycle breaks, I help them learn to talk about the things that matter without fighting. Underlying all of it is the same longing: a happy love relationship. So from the beginning I give couples strategies that renew hope, not just relief.

I’m a tour guide. I help people move through conflict toward more satisfying relationships, and I’m delighted to share what I know in a uniquely practical way. I’m married to Lois, and we enjoy life with our children and grandchildren.

Questions

The awkward questions.

The ones you’d rather not ask out loud. If yours isn’t here, email me. I answer my own mail.

Is this counseling or coaching?

Both are available. I’m a counselor in private practice, and the coaching element of my courses is an alternative to couples counseling. The content is the same material I walk couples through every week. If you want a licensed counselor in the room, that’s counseling. If you want the skills without the clinical frame, that’s coaching.

We’re not married. Does that matter?

No. Most of what breaks down is communication, not paperwork. Come as you are.

Can you take court-mandated anger management?

Yes. I work with self-mandated, corporate and court-mandated clients. I’m a Certified Anger Management Specialist–II (CAMS-2).

Do we have to live in your city?

No. Sessions run over video, and clients have joined from overseas across a seven-hour time difference. Wherever you are, we’ll find a time that works.

What does it cost?

Fees, formats and what’s included get covered on the first call, before you commit to anything. The three gift PDFs and the six tools are free and stay free.

What if my partner won’t come?

Start anyway. The courses and the guides are built to be used by one person, and plenty of couples begin with one person changing their side of the loop.
