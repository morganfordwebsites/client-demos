Pricing

What the fee is made of.

Virginia sets the most a notary may charge for each notarial act. Everything else is agreed with you before LaToya gets in the car, so the total is never a surprise at the door.

Notarial act
Up to $10 each

Each acknowledgment, oath or affirmation, affidavit or certified copy. The ceiling is set by Code of Virginia § 47.1-19.

Travel to you
Agreed in advance

Virginia allows a notary to recover actual, reasonable travel costs when you agree to them first. LaToya confirms the amount for your address before the visit.

Loan signing, refinance or HELOC
Flat fee, quoted

One fee for the whole package: printing, the signing appointment and returning the documents. Quoted when your booking is confirmed.

After-hours appointment
Additional fee

Early mornings and late evenings outside regular hours are available on request for an added fee, quoted with your confirmation.

    Questions

    Before you book.

    What ID do I need?

    A current government-issued photo ID for every person signing, such as a driver's license, state ID or passport. If a signer's ID has expired or the name does not match the document, mention it in the booking notes so LaToya can tell you what will work.

    Should I sign the document before she arrives?

    No. Complete the form but leave the signature blank. A notarization requires that you sign, or acknowledge your signature, in front of the notary.

    How much will it cost?

    Virginia caps the notarial fee at $10 per act. Travel is an actual, reasonable cost that you agree to in advance. Loan signings, refinances and HELOCs are one flat fee per package. LaToya emails the final price when she confirms your booking, before anyone travels.

    Can I book outside regular hours?

    Yes. Tick the after-hours box in the booking form to see early morning and late evening times. After-hours appointments carry an additional fee, quoted with your confirmation.

    Can LaToya explain my documents or tell me what to sign?

    She can point to where signatures, dates and initials go. She is not an attorney, so she cannot explain legal effect, choose a form for you or give legal advice. Questions about a loan go to your lender or title company.

    What if my document needs witnesses?

    Some documents require witnesses in addition to the notary. Please arrange them yourself and note it when booking, so enough time is set aside.
