How it works

From first call to finished report.

Four steps, no surprises in between. If the property is in escrow, give me the deadline and I will work to it.

Step one

Call or text

Send the property address and your timeline. I'll confirm a time that fits your agent's schedule.

Step two

On site

The property is walked room by room, roof to foundation, photographing what matters along the way. Join me if you can.

Step three

The report

A digital report organized system by system, with a photo of every finding so you see exactly what I saw.

Step four

The walkthrough call

We go through the findings together, in English or 中文, before you decide anything.
